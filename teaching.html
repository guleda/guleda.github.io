Courses taught

Undergraduate

BBY 167Bilgi Yönetimi ve Matematik

Examines logic and proofs, sets, functions, sequences and matrices in relation to information management. The course also covers algorithms, number theory, cryptography, counting, permutations, combinations and probability.

BBY 252Araştırma Yöntemleri

Covers the foundations of research, research questions and hypotheses, measurement, sampling, and qualitative and quantitative methods. Students also practise organising, visualising and analysing data in JASP and reporting research findings.

BBY 375Bilginin Görselleştirilmesi ve Sunumu

Develops skills in reading, assessing and creating tables and visualisations. Topics include visualisation types, infographics and presentations, visualising bibliometric data and literature, and reporting research findings.

BBY 458Araştırma Raporu

Students develop a research question, choose suitable methods and data collection techniques, and conduct an empirical study. The course covers literature review, sampling, data analysis, interpretation, reporting and presentation.

Master’s

BBY 606Araştırma Yöntemleri

Covers research design, methods and publication ethics, including research questions and hypotheses, measurement, sampling, quantitative analysis and statistical tests. Students also develop research proposals and reports.

BAR 607Bilgi Yöneticileri için Araştırma Yöntemleri

Examines the research process and qualitative and quantitative methods in information management. Topics include research proposals and design, data collection, statistical software, research ethics and reporting.

BAR 614Bibliyometri ve Sosyal Ağ Analizi

Introduces concepts, methods and applications in bibliometrics and social network analysis. Topics include citation and co-authorship analysis, data sources and software, alternative metrics and mapping research fields.

Doctoral

BBY 702Bilimsel İletişim

Examines the processes, outputs and actors of scholarly communication, including academic and electronic publishing, peer review and open access. It also covers copyright and licensing, ethics, disciplinary differences and emerging trends.

BBY 704Bibliyometri

Examines the history, concepts and methods of bibliometrics, including citation analysis, research performance indicators, bibliometric laws and data sources. The course also covers bibliometric software and social network analysis.
